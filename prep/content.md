## Overview/Introduction/Context
As documents and laws continue to be created at an increasing rate, legal and regulatory systems become more complex and difficult to navigate. This ever-growing body of rules can create confusion not only for ordinary citizens, but also for businesses and even legal professionals who must interpret and apply them correctly. Over time, outdated, overlapping, or contradictory laws can accumulate, making compliance more challenging and increasing the risk of mistakes or unfair enforcement. Because of this, there is a clear need for better systems to manage and organize legal information. Governments and institutions should regularly review existing laws to remove redundancies and update regulations so they remain relevant to modern society. In addition, technological solutions such as digital databases, search tools, and artificial intelligence can play an important role in making legal information more accessible and easier to understand.
